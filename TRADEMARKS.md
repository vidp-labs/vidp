# Vidp Trademark and Branding Policy

This policy explains how the Vidp name and brand assets may be used. It does
not grant ownership of the Vidp brand or change the repository license.

## Brand assets

The following identify the official Vidp project and official Vidp builds:

- the name **Vidp** and the Vidp logo;
- Vidp app icons and other official visual identity; and
- official screenshots, artwork, and promotional materials published by Vidp.

These assets were created for the Vidp project by Md Mostafijur Rahman. This
policy applies whether or not a mark is registered.

## Official builds

An official Vidp build is published by the `vidplabs` organization through
the Vidp repository's releases or the official Homebrew tap. A modified build,
fork, or third-party package is not an official Vidp build.

## Allowed references

You may make accurate, non-misleading factual references such as:

- "compatible with Vidp";
- "based on vidp-engine"; or
- "built from the Vidp source release" when that is true.

Keep the required copyright, license, and attribution notices with the work.

## Not allowed without permission

Do not use the Vidp name, logo, icons, or official artwork to:

- suggest that a modified app, fork, package, or service is official;
- suggest endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with Vidp;
- name or brand a modified or competing video-player product; or
- publish an altered app using Vidp's official identity.

A modified or rebranded build must use a distinct name, icon, and visual
identity. It must also clearly identify itself as unofficial.

The `vidp-engine` name may be used to identify that an application consumes
the engine, but that use does not grant permission to present the application
as the official Vidp app.

For GPL combined works, this policy does not change the permissions in
[LICENSE section 4](LICENSE); it governs only the Vidp brand assets.

For permission questions, open an issue in the [Vidp repository](https://github.com/vidplabs/vidp/issues).
